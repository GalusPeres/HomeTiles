# <img src="docs/images/favicon.svg" width="42" alt="" align="top"> HomeTiles

Touch dashboards for Home Assistant on ESP32-P4 and ESP32-S3 displays.

[**Documentation**](https://galusperes.github.io/) · [**Online flasher**](https://galusperes.github.io/installer/) · [**Latest release**](https://github.com/GalusPeres/HomeTiles/releases/latest)

</div>

Control lights, covers, heating and music, or display sensors, energy statistics and weather. Arrange tiles and folders in the built-in Web Admin, with a live preview and automatic saving. A configurable screensaver adds a clock, photos and tiles.

**New in [v0.6.10](docs/releases/v0.6.10.md):** Number, Select and Date/Time controls with history, display navigation from Home Assistant, two-line titles, Waveshare LCD-4 Rev 4.0 support, and the hardware-confirmed Guition JC8012 V1 Camera/Web OTA transport fix.

## Get started

You need a compatible display, Home Assistant, an MQTT broker and [HomeTiles Bridge](https://github.com/GalusPeres/HomeTiles-Bridge).

1. Check your exact model, revision and known limitations in the [device list](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support).
2. Install with the [online flasher](https://galusperes.github.io/installer/).
3. Follow [Home Assistant setup](https://galusperes.github.io/home-assistant-setup/), then [create your dashboard](https://galusperes.github.io/web-admin/).

Camera tiles are experimental and available on ESP32-P4 only. Device-specific fixes and test builds are linked from the device list.
