# HomeTiles v0.7.0

v0.7.0 adds flexible tile layouts and colors, shares built-in display cameras with Home Assistant, and improves Settings editing and folder-cache memory use.

## Highlights

- **Half-step layouts:** move and resize tiles in 0.5-cell steps, with compact half-height tiles for supported types. Settings and Back tiles support sizes down to 1x0.5. Climate mini-tiles keep their positions while they still fit after resizing.
- **A configurable look:** adjust corner radius, icon circles, icon colors and tile colors. Rules can follow a tile's own entity or another entity, with colors based on values or states. Clock, Text and Back tiles can hide their borders individually.
- **Built-in cameras:** compatible ESP32-P4 displays can share snapshots and live video with Home Assistant or another HomeTiles display. Cameras are off by default; enable them in Web Admin. Recording indicators, rotation and image controls are included. Camera support remains experimental.
- **Clearer graphs and controls:** Sensor and Energy graphs show the value under your finger. Climate supports the fan and swing mode names supplied by Home Assistant, including horizontal swing.
- **Web Admin fixes:** selecting Settings after a Folder clears the folder-only hint. Hidden Settings tiles retain their size when restored. Copy/paste requires a fitting empty area and cannot replace Settings or Back tiles.
- **More memory for folders:** processed Media, Weather, Climate, Cover and Switch updates now release their message buffers instead of retaining them in empty queue slots.
- **Device and usability updates:** Guition V2 touch bounds, internal I2C allocation and SD cleanup guards; Tab5 wake brightness; Wi-Fi list and AP-mode popup fixes; and screensaver image lists that stay in sync with the SD card.
- **Additional profiles and documentation:** Guition JC4880P443 portrait support, a separate Waveshare 10.1 image for ESP32-P4 v3.1 or newer, and refreshed feature guides and screenshots.

## Update Notes

1. Update to **[HomeTiles Bridge v0.7.0](https://github.com/GalusPeres/HomeTiles-Bridge/releases/tag/v0.7.0)** through HACS and restart Home Assistant before updating displays.
2. **Export your dashboard first** using Web Admin **Import / Export**, and keep the JSON file. The export includes tiles, folders and the screensaver layout; it excludes local I/O assignments and PINs.
3. Use the exact device's regular **`.bin`** for OTA, or choose **Update** in the browser installer. Normal updates preserve settings. **`_factory.bin`** and **First install / factory reset** are only for first installation or an intentional reset.

Choose the exact model and silicon revision. Guition JC8012 V1 and V2 use different images. Waveshare 10.1 pre-v3 and v3.1-or-newer images are separate; the experimental Waveshare 7B v3.1 image is for exact v3.1 only.

**Going back to v0.6.x:** first make every tile's position and size whole, including Settings, folder Back tiles, Climate tiles and screensaver tiles, and remove overlaps. Export again if you need a backup that can be imported in v0.6.x, then upload the older model's regular `.bin`. Older firmware does not understand half-size tiles. Follow [Going back to v0.6.x](https://galusperes.github.io/updating/#going-back-to-v06x) before downgrading.

Colors, rules, icon circles, corner radius and camera settings need no downgrade preparation. Older firmware ignores them; they return on v0.7.0 if the tiles keep their places in between.

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer tested the release candidate on **M5Stack Tab5, Waveshare LCD-4B (P4), Waveshare LCD-8 (P4), Guition JC8012P4A1 V2 and Guition ESP32-4848S040 (S3)** and reports stable operation so far, including the S3.

Built-in cameras were tested on Guition JC8012P4A1 V2, Waveshare LCD-8 and M5Stack Tab5. The V2 cache-fix log recorded 76 cached folder switches without eviction or preload rollback; the final 20 reported 124 KiB free heap.

Contributor testing established the original JC4880P443 profile and camera support, and the Waveshare 10.1 v3 profile on v3.2 hardware. Validation of these final release images remains pending.

## Pending Hardware Validation

Built-in camera testing for the release images remains pending on Waveshare 7, 10.1, 7B, 4.3 and 4B, Guition JC8012 V1, JC1060 V2 and JC4880. Exact Waveshare 7B v3.1 hardware, broader community-board testing, and long-running camera, sleep/wake and memory checks remain open. See the [device support notes](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support) for individual limitations.

The candidate passed all 17 CI build variants for 15 physical device profiles. Local tests passed 173/173; CI passed 152 with 21 optional-tool skips. These checks establish build and regression coverage, not untested hardware behavior. The cache fix does not establish the cause of every previous low-memory crash.

Thanks to **damianeek** for JC4880P443 support, **memooox3** for the Waveshare 10.1 v3 contribution, and everyone testing HomeTiles and reporting device behavior.
