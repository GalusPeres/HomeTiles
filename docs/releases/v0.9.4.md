# HomeTiles v0.9.4

v0.9.4 rebuilds the media player popup, turns the display's speaker into a small player and lets Home Assistant set the theme and the rotation.

## Highlights

- Media popup: two layouts per tile (cover on the left or in the middle), and only the controls the player offers in Home Assistant. Shuffle and repeat show in the icon's color with a dot when they are on. The time bar follows the position Home Assistant reports and no longer jumps back when the volume changes.
- A menu in the popup's corner opens the player's **Speakers** (play on several speakers together), its **Source** and its **Library** to browse and play media. The library keeps its place until the popup closes. The speakers tab shows how many players play together.
- Media tile: the icon shows whether the player plays, pauses or is off, and the playing bars from the popup stand over the title.
- Speaker on ESP32-P4 displays with voice: it plays every format Home Assistant sends (the Bridge decodes it), with title, position, pause and seeking in Home Assistant. A camera stream with sound plays its sound in the camera popup and full screen, in step with the picture. **Settings → Display** has a **Sound** group with volume and a microphone switch (#123). On a new display the microphone and wake word listening start switched off.
- Home Assistant: the display's device page has a **Theme** select (Dark, Light, Automatic) and a **Display Rotation** select with the steps the display offers (0°, 90°, 180°, 270° on displays that turn in quarter turns, 0° and 180° on the others).
- Add a display to Home Assistant by its IP address where mDNS does not reach it (#114).
- Web Admin: a **+** on a free page field makes a new page (#100), and a deleted folder leaves the display and its page at once. The export contains the Settings tile, the import shows its progress to the end, and the display always starts with the chosen layout.
- Fixes: units with "µ" show the micro sign (#115); ESP32-P4 displays give each Wi-Fi join 30 s and retry 5 s after a failed one (#120); MP3 clips that crashed the speaker play or are skipped (#119); the Waveshare 7B v3 runs its Wi-Fi link at 20 MHz and restarts cleanly; lists in the first-start setup scroll by touch (#41); the GS-T3E keeps all three relays (#93).

## Update Notes

Update HomeTiles Bridge to v0.9.4 first: the media library, speaker streaming, camera sound and the Theme and Display Rotation selects need it. With an older Bridge the display works as before.

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.
