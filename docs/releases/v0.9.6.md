# HomeTiles v0.9.6

v0.9.6 adds the Calendar and Power flow tiles, graphs on Sensor and Energy tiles, new Sensor looks, Russian as the eighth language and faster drawing on every display.

## Highlights

- Calendar tile (#84): agenda, countdown, week and month views and a calendar popup with a month view and a list of the coming four weeks. Several calendars in one tile, each with its own color.
- Power flow tile: solar, grid, battery and home power as nodes with moving dots that follow the power's direction and size. The battery shows its charge and whether it charges; tap a node for its sensor popup.
- Sensor graph (#131): the area under the line fades from the icon's color, the tile shows the last 24 hours and the popup has the ranges **30D**, **7D**, **24H**, **6H** and **1H**. Touch the graph to read an earlier value.
- Energy popup: **Year**, **Month**, **Week** and **Today**, each from its start up to now, with bars in the icon's color. The Energy tile can show today's hours as bars (**Display mode: Graph**). **Web Admin → Localization → First day of the week** sets where a week starts for Energy, the Calendar and the 30D graph lines.
- Sensor tile looks: the value alone, a bar or a ring, each filled, as a line or striped, open or closed, with the value on top or inside. The editor opens them in a menu like the Switch tile's.
- Rules can change the icon (#102): every color in a rule can bring its own icon, and the tile's own icon comes back when the rule ends.
- Switch and Fan tiles can stand their bar upright (**Orientation: Upright**).
- Lights (#130): the Home Assistant color in the dark and the light theme, and **Fill** also fills white and near-white light. A Switch tile keeps the level set in the Light popup while Home Assistant answers.
- Russian: all texts, Cyrillic in every text size and a ЙЦУКЕН keyboard whose **ABC** key switches to QWERTY for passwords.
- Units stand a step smaller after their number, like in the weather forecast, on tiles, in popups and in state lines. The weather tile fits one more forecast day.
- Faster: fonts unpack each character once, rounded corners and shadows come from kept masks, page turns skip tiles that did not change, the pages overview redraws only its card, and the Guition JC8012P4A1 V2 copies a finished part of the screen while the next one is drawn. Popups open in their tile's colors from the first frame.
- Guition JC8012P4A1 V1 and V2: a resting finger holds still and a slow drag stays close to the finger; a lifting finger no longer moves a slider at the last moment.
- Web Admin: value size lists name the sizes the display really draws, and the head bar page dots follow the preview's theme.
- New image: the Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-4.3 with ESP32-P4 v3.1 or newer (experimental, #135). Check the revision with `esptool chip-id` and choose **Waveshare Touch LCD 4.3 inch (ESP32-P4 v3.1 or newer, experimental)** in the browser installer.

## Update Notes

Update HomeTiles Bridge to v0.9.6 first: the Calendar tile's events, the Energy popup's Year and Week and the Sensor graph's 30D and 1H need it. Choose the calendars for the Calendar tile in the Bridge's entity configuration (**Calendars**).

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer tested the test builds of this release on the Guition JC8012P4A1 V2 and the Guition ESP32-4848S040: the Power flow tile, the Sensor graph with its five ranges, the Energy popup periods and Energy tile bars, and the faster drawing and page turns. The Calendar tile was contributor-tested on an ESP32-4848S040C_I panel.

## Pending Hardware Validation

The Calendar tile still needs a check on the maintainer's displays. The Russian texts and how they fit on each display need feedback from Russian speakers. The Sensor looks, rule icons, upright Switch and Fan bars and the light colors are new; please report what you notice. The Waveshare 4.3-inch v3 image is not tested on hardware yet. The other boards need field feedback as before. See the [device support notes](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support).
