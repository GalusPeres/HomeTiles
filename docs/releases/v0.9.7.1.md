# HomeTiles v0.9.7.1

v0.9.7.1 adds the Weather graph tile, lets the screensaver use the whole screen and translates the Yandex weather conditions, the wind unit and the one-line clock options.

## Highlights

- New **Weather graph** tile: a linked pair of tiles for Today and Tomorrow of one weather entity, a smooth temperature line, the condition band below (no numeric precipitation from the free Yandex API), labels every three hours and a dashed marker with its dot on the current hour of the Today half. Each half shows the tile's own Name, otherwise Today and Tomorrow. Past hours come from the panel's own hourly history, so the day is whole from the start.
- Screensaver: the tiles may use the whole screen (no rows are reserved under the head), the graph lays out from the card's real size, and a tap on a tile without an action (Sensor, Energy, Binary sensor, Cover, Weather graph, Number, Select, Date) leaves the screensaver for the panel.
- The screensaver clock can sit on one line (time beside the date), so the overlay fits the very top of the screen. New checkbox in the clock settings with the same gap and alignment as the Web Admin preview.
- Yandex Pogoda conditions are translated everywhere: 32 keys (Home Assistant's fifteen plus Yandex's own, including underscored states like `light_rain`, `sleet` and `snowfall`) on the weather tile and popup, on condition sensors, in the condition band labels and in the Web Admin.
- Units follow the language: the wind speed shows `м/с` in Russian and `m/s` in the other languages, on sensor tiles, in the sensor popup and in the Web Admin.
- Negative values show a minus again at the big value font sizes 80 and 96 (the glyph was missing from those two fonts).
- BTT Panda Touch: the pixel clock drops to 6 MHz while the blocking firmware update check runs and is restored afterwards, like the Guition profiles.

## Update Notes

No Bridge update needed: this release works with HomeTiles Bridge v0.9.7. Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer tested this release on the BTT Panda Touch: the Weather graph pair on the panel and on the screensaver, the screensaver's whole-screen tiles and the inert-tile tap, the one-line clock with its gap and alignment, the translated Yandex conditions, the `м/с` unit and the minus at the value sizes 80 and 96.

## Pending Hardware Validation

The Weather graph tile, the screensaver changes and the translated conditions still need field feedback on the other displays, the ESP32-P4 boards in particular. The Panda Touch update-check pixel-clock guard is validated on one panel; the other boards need field feedback as before. See the [device support notes](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support).
