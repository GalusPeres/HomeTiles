# HomeTiles v0.9.7

v0.9.7 adds the Vacuum tile, keeps the Energy tile's bars current during the hour and brings an image for the Waveshare 8-inch with ESP32-P4 v3.

## Highlights

- Vacuum tile (#50): the state and battery, and buttons by the tile's width: **Clean** (**Pause** while cleaning) on one column, **Clean** and **Dock** on two, **Clean**, **Stop** and **Dock** from three. From two by two the tile shows the vacuum's map with its state, battery and cleaned area beside it. Only the buttons the vacuum supports are active.
- Vacuum popup: the map (left or centred), cleaned area, cleaning time and battery, **Clean**, **Stop** and **Dock**, and a menu with the suction power, the rooms to clean and **Locate**.
- The vacuum's map comes from the image or camera entity of its device, or the one you choose in the tile; a camera map refreshes at the tile's **Refresh (s)**. The map is fetched from the start and shows at once in the tile and the popup.
- Energy tile bars and today's total follow the running hour instead of standing still until the next full hour (with HomeTiles Bridge v0.9.7).
- Sensor popup (#139): the graph opens with the newest point as a dot in the line's color; the cursor line appears once you touch the graph.
- Button tile (#138): buttons with long entity ids work; the picker keeps the full entity id.
- New image: the Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-8 with ESP32-P4 v3.1 or newer (experimental, #140). Check the revision with `esptool chip-id` and choose **Waveshare Touch LCD 8 inch (ESP32-P4 v3.1 or newer, experimental)** in the browser installer.

## Update Notes

Update HomeTiles Bridge to v0.9.7 first: the Vacuum tile and the Energy bars of the running hour need it. Choose the vacuums in the Bridge's entity configuration (**Vacuums**). Cleaning single rooms needs Home Assistant 2026.3 or newer and the area mapping in the vacuum's entity settings.

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer tested the test builds of this release on the Guition JC8012P4A1 V2 with a vacuum that has a camera map: the Vacuum tile in its sizes, the popup, the buttons and the map.

## Pending Hardware Validation

The Vacuum tile and popup still need a check on the other displays, the 480×480 panels in particular. The Energy bars of the running hour, the Sensor popup's dot and the Button fix need field feedback. The Waveshare 8-inch v3 image is not tested on hardware yet. The other boards need field feedback as before. See the [device support notes](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support).
