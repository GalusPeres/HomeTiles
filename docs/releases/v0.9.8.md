# HomeTiles v0.9.8

v0.9.8 turns the Camera tile into a tile for pictures from several sources, Home Assistant cameras, a rain radar and webcams, and lets the screensaver's tiles work like the pages, with popups and frosted glass.

## Highlights

- **Camera / Picture tile:** each tile has a **Source**: **Home Assistant** (cameras and images), **Rain radar** or **Webcam**, with up to eight entries. The popup's **…** button switches between them; **Tile shows** picks the entry on the tile, or **Last chosen**.
- **Rain radar:** for Home Assistant's home, a zone, a town by name or your own coordinates, with nothing to set up. The popup plays the frames with **Play** and a time bar, and the head shows **Synced 10:20**. An emergency **Radar source** is on the System page.
- **Webcams:** the address of a webcam picture or of a webcam's own page. New pictures come every 10 minutes at fixed times and stay for a day. Foto-Webcam and snow-online bring their last day at once.
- **Camera popup:** no status words over the picture, the last picture shows at once, and after a lost connection to the Bridge it continues by itself. Only a connection gone for 30 seconds is shown.
- **Screensaver tiles work like the pages:** a tap opens their popup over the screensaver while the slides pause, Sensor tiles show their graph, gauge or look, and Energy tiles their bars. Tiles and popups follow the screensaver's own Dark or Light design.
- **Frosted glass:** see-through screensaver tiles show the slide blurred behind them.
- **Against image retention:** the screensaver's clock and tiles move a few pixels now and then (#111).
- **Energy tiles** and an open Energy popup refresh every five minutes, like Home Assistant's energy dashboard.
- **Time zone Automatic** takes Home Assistant's time zone (#145); Alaska is new in the list.
- **Popups** close with a tap beside the card, like the X (#142).
- **Sensor tiles:** an empty **Decimals** field no longer turns into 0, so text values stay whole (#157).
- **Graphs:** graph tiles take turns and stay current; an open Sensor popup's graph follows the time.
- **Fixes:** tiles could stand still overnight until the next touch; a Media tile on the screensaver could restart the panel; an automatic title could show a vacuum's name twice; the Web Admin no longer lets a tile type without room cover its neighbours.

## Update Notes

Update HomeTiles Bridge to v0.9.8 first and restart Home Assistant: rain radar, webcams, the time bar and **Time zone Automatic** need it.

Webcams: their pictures belong to the webcam's operator. Show only pictures the site allows you to use; some portals ask for consent first, some do not allow it at all. A site that blocks automatic requests is named in the Web Admin; where the site allows its pictures to be used, paste the address of the picture itself.

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer used the test builds of this release for two weeks on the Guition JC8012P4A1 V2, the Guition ESP32-4848S040 and the Waveshare 8-inch: the Camera tile with rain radar, webcams and Home Assistant cameras, the screensaver's popups and frosted glass, and the Energy refresh.

## Pending Hardware Validation

The Camera tile's sources, the screensaver popups and the frosted glass need field feedback on the other displays. ESP32-S3 displays show camera pictures at about 4 frames per second. The other boards need field feedback as before. See the [device support notes](https://galusperes.github.io/#device-support).
